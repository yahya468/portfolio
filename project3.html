Explore Egypt: Egyptian Food

The food section of my Explore Egypt project.

About this project

This is the food section of my Explore Egypt project. It introduces Egyptian dishes such as Ful Medames and Kushari with headings, descriptions and space for pictures. My aim was to share a little about Egyptian food in a simple page.

What I learned

I practised putting information into separate sections and arranging pictures beside text. This helped me learn how to make a page easier to read.
