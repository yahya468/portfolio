Explore Egypt: Must-Visit Destinations

The destinations section of my Explore Egypt project.

About this project

This is another part of my Explore Egypt project, focused on must-visit destinations. The page includes sections about the Great Pyramids of Giza and Khan el-Khalili Bazaar. My aim was to introduce places in Egypt using a simple layout.

What I learned

I practised using consistent headings, pictures and descriptions across a page. I also learned how keeping a similar layout can connect different parts of the same project.
